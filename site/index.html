WHAT YOU GET

Strict mode

The default. It holds you to the game's connectivity rules: you can only dig down where there is an elevator, and you cannot delete a room that would cut others off from the vault door.

Free mode

Switches those two rules off so you can sketch in any order and fill the elevators in later. Geometry still holds: rooms never overlap and never run past the edge of a floor.

Save, share, export

Keep a layout as a JSON file, copy a link that carries the whole vault, or export the plan as a PNG. There is no server holding your layout — the link is the layout.

What it checks while you build

Every problem is listed in a panel of its own as you go, and each one takes you to the room it is about. Nothing is silently corrected.

  • Rooms stay on the grid. No overlap, and nothing runs past the 26 cells of a floor.
  • A floor needs a shaft. You can only dig down where an elevator already reaches.
  • Every room keeps a route home. Deleting one that would strand the rest from the vault door is refused.
  • Merges line up. Like joins like, up to three builds wide, and a merged room is deleted whole unless you split it first.
  • Sizes come from the game. Each room's width and population requirement are the real ones.
  • S.P.E.C.I.A.L. is shown, not enforced. The stat sits beside each room in the palette; it rules nothing.

Why plan a vault at all?

In Fallout Shelter a room is easy to build and awkward to undo. Elevators decide which floors you can reach, merged rooms come out whole, and a layout you dug in the first hour is the one you live with at a hundred dwellers.

This planner is the cheap place to make those mistakes. Tear a floor out, try the other arrangement, undo it, and keep the version you liked as a file or a link — before you spend a cap on it.

Questions

Is this an official tool?
No. It is an unofficial fan project, not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Bethesda Softworks.
Do I need an account?
No. There is nothing to sign up for, and no server that keeps your vault. The editing happens in your browser, and a layout only ever exists in a file you save and in the URL you share.
Where do the room facts come from?
Sizes, S.P.E.C.I.A.L. stats and unlock requirements are taken from the Fallout Wiki. No artwork from the game is used — every glyph here is drawn from plain shapes.
